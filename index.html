01 Bloom BL-3: Apply

Q1: Calculate percentage of accredited colleges

Computed exact proportion of accredited institutions: 67.06% (4,694 of 7,000).

Collapse Answer ↑

Objective & Theory

Calculate what percentage of the 7,000 institutions are classified as Accredited within the categorical variable Accreditation_Status, while reporting the complete three-category distribution.

Mathematical Formula

$$ \text{Percentage of Accredited Colleges} = \left( \frac{n_{\text{accredited}}}{N} \right) \times 100 $$

Where $n_{\text{accredited}} = 4,694$ (numerator), $N = 7,000$ (denominator). Result $= 67.0571\% \approx 67.06\%$.

Execution Code

total_colleges = len(df)
accredited_count = int((df["Accreditation_Status"] == "Accredited").sum())
accredited_percentage = (accredited_count / total_colleges) * 100
# Output: 67.06%

03 Bloom BL-4: Analyze

Q3: Fill missing accreditation rating data

Diagnosed 866 missing ratings (12.37%) and applied a principled two-stage group-wise median imputation.

View Answer →

Problem Diagnosis & Cleaning Theory

Accreditation_Rating had 866 missing values (12.37%). Observed ratings range from 0.53 to 4.00, with an observed mean of 2.6351, median of 2.8500, and moderate left-skewness of -0.7544.

❌ Why Zero-Fill was Rejected: The minimum observed rating is 0.53. Filling missing scores with 0 treats "unknown" as severe institutional failure and manufactures an artificial zero spike.
❌ Why Listwise Deletion was Rejected: Dropping rows would permanently purge 866 colleges (12.37%), including 233 accredited colleges with complete geographic data.

Mathematical Formulation (Sample Mean)

$$ \bar{x} = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n} x_i}{n} $$

Where $\bar{x} = 2.6351$ is the observed sample mean, $x_i$ represents each observed rating score, and $n = 6,134$ is the count of observed non-missing ratings.

Methodology: Two-Stage Group-Wise Median

  • Stage 1 (Grade Median): Ratings are tightly clustered by grade (A++ median = 3.75; D median = 1.07). Missing ratings with a known grade are filled with their respective grade median. This resolved 737 missing values.
  • Stage 2 (Status Fallback): 129 colleges lacked both rating and grade. For these, fallback medians were calculated across Accreditation_Status (Accredited: 3.03, Not Accredited: 1.34, Under Review: 2.72). This resolved the remaining 129 values to 0 missing.

Before vs. After Comparison

Stage Missing Count Missing % Mean Score Median Score
Observed Only (Before) 866 12.37% 2.6351 2.8500
After Two-Stage Imputation 0 0.00% 2.5584 2.7900

Academic Caveat

Imputed values are statistical estimates, not real-world measurements. A college assigned 1.34 because it was unaccredited did not "earn" 1.34; it received the synthetic cohort median.

Theory & Mathematical Formulation

Grouping partitions the DataFrame into subsets sharing distinct values of state $s$ and grade $g$, and counts rows via size():

$$ \operatorname{Count}(\text{State} = s, \, \text{Grade} = g) = \# \{ \text{colleges with State } s \text{ and Grade } g \} $$

Where $s \in \{1, \dots, 36\}$ observed states and $g \in \{\text{A++}, \text{A+}, \text{A}, \text{B++}, \text{B+}, \text{B}, \text{C}, \text{D}\}$ quality grades.

Rows missing either State (175) or Grade (608) total 776 rows and are excluded from the default frequency groupby.
